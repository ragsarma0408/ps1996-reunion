About the Reunion

Three decades after we walked the corridors of P.S. Senior Secondary School, it’s time to reconnect. Whether you can join us in Chennai or from across the world, this site is our shared space to plan, vote, share memories, and volunteer.

  • Register your participation (in-person or virtual)
  • Vote on preferred weekend in December 2026 (cutoff 30 Sep 2026)
  • Vote on evening format – resort dinner/party or short ocean cruise
  • Share a quick memory and a photo for the Class Collage
  • Step up as a volunteer for Morning, Evening, or Treasurer roles

Volunteer Roles

Self-nominate for one of the three key roles. A photo thumbnail of volunteers will be shown so the group can see who is stepping up.

🏫

Morning Event Coordinator

Connects with teachers & staff to confirm space and teacher participation for the 30-year reunion, including catering for lunch at school.

🎉

Evening Event Coordinator

Identifies a resort with event space for casual catch-up, dinner, party, dance / music / DJ, or arranges the cruise option.

💰

Treasurer

Collects contributions from in-person participants, accepts donations from virtual participants, pays expenses, and purchases memorabilia.
